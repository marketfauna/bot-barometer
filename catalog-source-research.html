When this research may be useful

There is a source decision to prepare

The work fits a catalog team comparing product identifiers, checking how an application claim is qualified, or investigating disagreement between manufacturer sources. We prepare the research; your team decides whether it supports release or needs escalation.

Your existing lookup may already be enough

If an authoritative supplier record already answers the question and your team can check it quickly, this service may add little. The first record lets you judge usefulness against your own lookup, explanation and review effort before commissioning more.

Review remains part of the work: your team checks the agreed identity, required fields, source support and exception dispositions. We do not promise a number of hours saved or a complete, ready-to-publish listing.

Review before paying.

These terms apply to this source-research pilot. Scope and timing are confirmed before you commit, and silence never creates a payment obligation.

  1. Agree the scope before work

    We propose the product/configuration, required fields, sources, output format, permitted unresolved fields and delivery date. You approve or trim that scope. We handle the agreed research and clarification with authorized sources; manufacturer response times cannot be promised.

  2. Deliver against the agreed criteria

    A record is complete when its identity and required fields meet the agreed source and finish criteria, the output opens in the agreed format, and material conflicts have the agreed disposition. An unexpected unresolved required field is incomplete work.

  3. Take five business days to review

    After delivery, you have five business days to explicitly accept the record or identify defects against those criteria. Your review decides whether the agreed research outcome has been delivered.

  4. One correction round is included

    We return corrections within five business days of receiving your defect list. You then have five business days to review the correction. A newer webpage alone does not automatically overrule an older official guide.

  5. Choose what happens to an unresolved field

    If a required field remains unresolved afterward, you may explicitly accept the documented hold at the full milestone fee, or decline that milestone with no fee and no obligation. We do not silently treat incomplete research as a successful delivery.

  6. Pay only after explicit acceptance

    We request US$50 after acceptance of the first record. Only if you commission the next four do we request US$200 after acceptance of those four. There is no obligation to order the additional records.

An unaccepted milestone closes unpaid after the applicable review/correction window. Later new work requires a new agreement. Additional products, changed requirements and source updates are separately scoped.
