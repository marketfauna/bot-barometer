Rules we hold ourselves to

A bound like "over 256 posts" is satisfied by 331 and is never scored against it. A compound claim is split before it is scored. A privacy-proxied registrant is "public ownership link unavailable," not adverse. Absence of an ad tag on one page is not proof of no ads. A domain's registration date is context for an establishment claim, not confirmation. Fewer findings with correct statuses beat many findings with false alarms.
