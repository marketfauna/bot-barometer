PROMPT: $10,000, one AI subscription with a weekly usage cap, a human who will only
sign documents and do nothing else. Thousands of other agents have the identical setup.
Legal only. Top 3 strategies, one line each.

FRAMING (the constraint that actually binds):
The scarce resource is not the $10k and not the tokens — it is the human's signature and
legal identity, and any durable edge must come from something the other thousand agents
cannot copy by reading the same prompt. So: avoid anything whose supply is "an agent with
a subscription" (content, SEO, generic SaaS, cold outreach, arbitrage of public prices) —
that supply goes to infinity this week and the price goes to zero. Buy or build assets
whose supply is fixed: verified identity, custody, a legal wrapper, a relationship, an
audit trail, escrowed money, an installed base.

TOP 3 STRATEGIES

1. Sell the bottleneck itself — become the notary/escrow layer for the other agents:
   use the human's signature and a real bank account to offer agent-to-agent escrow,
   incorporation filings, contract counter-signature and identity attestation, priced per
   transaction, because thousands of agents all need a legal person and almost none of
   them can be one.

2. Buy a small existing cash-flowing asset rather than starting one — spend $6-8k
   acquiring a neglected micro-business, aged domain, niche newsletter list, or
   maintenance-abandoned paid plugin with real revenue and real customers, then use the
   token budget on operations the seller was too bored to do, because a customer list is
   the one input the swarm cannot generate from a prompt.

3. Run the deterministic, capital-consuming half of a business that agents structurally
   avoid — inventory, physical fulfillment, licensed/regulated services under the human's
   credentials, or paid-for-cash data collection — and sell that capability to the swarm,
   because when a thousand competitors are all long on intelligence and short on money,
   permits and hands, the return accrues to whoever is short on intelligence and long on
   everything else.

TIEBREAKER / SEQUENCING
Week 1: spend tokens on diligence, not production — find which of the three has a buyer
who already pays money today. Cap any single irreversible commitment at ~$2k until one
real customer has paid, because the failure mode for a swarm agent is not picking wrong,
it is spending the whole $10k before learning anything. Keep the human's signature rate-
limited and logged: it is the only asset that cannot be replenished if misused.

WHAT I WOULD NOT DO
Content farms, affiliate sites, dropshipping, prompt/agent marketplaces, "AI consulting"
cold email, trading. All of these are the modal answer, which is exactly why their
expected value is negative once a thousand agents run them simultaneously.
