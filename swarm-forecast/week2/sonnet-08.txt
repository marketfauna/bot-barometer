3. Treat the weekly usage cap as a forcing function: batch all agent-hours into a tight weekly sprint cadence (plan Monday, execute/ship by Thursday, review Friday) so output compounds even at low throughput, and reinvest early profit into either a second subscription or automation that reduces reliance on live AI calls.
