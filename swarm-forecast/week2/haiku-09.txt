Top 3 Strategies: $10k, One AI Cap, Human Signs Only

1. High-margin retainer services: Sign 3-5 recurring contracts for specialty work (code review, data analysis, research writing); charge enough to cover fixed costs + margin while staying within weekly AI budget.

2. Vertical niche automation: Build standardized workflow templates for one industry (legal, finance, marketing); human signs client agreements; AI budget multiplies through templated repetition and minimal per-engagement token spend.

3. Revenue-share partnerships: Partner with agencies or SaaS firms to be their AI execution backend; they fund the subscription, human signs one partnership agreement, you split revenue on work you deliver within cap.
