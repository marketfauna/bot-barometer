VOREC Community Grant 2027: pre-application package

Pre-applications due by email: 11:59 PM Eastern, Sunday 13 December 2026

If your town, regional commission or nonprofit plans a 2027 VOREC Community Grant for outdoor recreation planning, design or promotion, we'll assemble the written pre-application from your records. You supply the project information, review our drafts, and submit the application.

What you get, for one project

  • A draft workplan on FPR's current 2027 template, assembled from your records with activities, results and proposed dates checked against the guidance.
  • A draft budget on FPR's current 2027 template, with itemized costs tied to the workplan and the amount requested from VCGP, checked against the guidance's grant range and cost rules.
  • Draft written form answers from your records, for you to edit and approve, including your project's supported connection to Move Forward Together Vermont's equity or climate-resilience objectives and relevant priority actions.
  • A check of your scope against this year's rules. The 2027 round funds outdoor recreation project development and promotion. The construction/maintenance exception is eligible wayfinding or informational sign/kiosk installation as a promotion activity; signs used only for navigation within a trail network are not funded by VCGP.
  • A short list of questions to put to FPR where your project's fit is unclear, so you can ask before you submit.

Check the fit before booking

Eligible applicants include municipalities, other governmental entities and nonprofits. If you still have an active VCGP grant on 13 December 2026, you generally cannot apply this round; the exclusion does not apply to entities serving exclusively as a fiscal sponsor on a current VCGP grant. Other FPR grants do not trigger that exclusion. Confirm your status with FPR.

The guidance recommends a minimum request of USD 7,500 and sets a USD 50,000 maximum, with no required match. VCGP is still a reimbursement program: an awarded applicant must finance eligible expenses first. The USD 450 application-preparation fee is separate from your grant request; expenses incurred before award are not reimbursable by VCGP.

What stays with you

You remain responsible for the project facts, approvals, signatures and submission, and for confirming your own eligibility with FPR. For kiosk/sign installation, site maps, applicable landowner-permission documents and permit reports come from you or your advisers; we do not produce or certify them. The guidance places these documents after project selection, during agreement development or an agreed checkpoint, with FPR approval before installation. They are not part of the written pre-application package. Technical design, site facts and professional judgments also remain with your qualified advisers. We never use your logins.

What we need from you

The project description and location, what the work would produce, cost estimates or quotes, any partners or contractors, and contact details. A one-page summary is enough to discuss fit. Before booking, we check the current form and templates, confirm that we can assemble and independently review the agreed written package, and agree the scope and complete list of inputs. If an essential input or current template is unavailable, we do not book a delivery we cannot support.

Timing

Send your project summary by Monday 30 November 2026 so we can assess fit and leave time for revisions. Our target turnaround is five working days after we confirm that the agreed inputs are complete. Before accepting the work, we confirm availability for both assembly and independent checking, and a delivery date, in writing. The 30 November date is our intake cutoff, separate from FPR's 13 December deadline.

What this isn't

It isn't a promise of an invitation or of funding; FPR decides both. The full application is a separate stage, by invitation, and is not included in this service.
