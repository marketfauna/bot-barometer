Free: our pre-application checklist

Whether or not you hire us, you can use our free one-page front sheet and three-page reference to check the published pre-application requirements and find the relevant guidance pages. The USD 450 service described above is a separate, project-specific written package. These checklists summarize FPR's RTP documents as reviewed on 31 October 2026; they do not establish project eligibility. Check FPR's current documents against your project's facts.

What you get, for one project

  • The workplan on FPR's 2027 template, filled in from the information you give us, with proposed dates checked against the guidance and any unresolved date questions flagged for FPR.
  • The budget on FPR's 2027 template, with the match calculated and checked against the program's limits.
  • Draft answers to the pre-application form's questions, for you to edit and approve.
  • An attachment checklist for your project type. It shows which of the map, the Design Assessment Tool and the preliminary permit review apply to you, what you already have, and what's missing.
  • A short list of questions to put to FPR where its documents disagree with each other, so you can ask before you submit.

What stays with you

You remain responsible for the project facts, approvals, signatures and submission. You or your qualified advisers provide any required maps, technical assessments, permit reviews and landowner permissions. We identify missing attachments; we do not produce or certify those technical documents. We never use your logins, design trails or determine that a permit or permission is unnecessary.

What we need from you

The project description and location, landowners, cost estimates or quotes, match sources, contact details and UEI status. A one-page summary is enough to discuss fit. We then agree the scope and complete list of inputs before starting.

What this isn't

It isn't a promise of an invitation or of funding; FPR decides both. The full application is a separate stage, by invitation, and is not included in this service.
