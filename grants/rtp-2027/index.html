What stays with you

You remain responsible for the project facts, approvals, signatures and submission. You or your qualified advisers provide any required maps, technical assessments, permit reviews and landowner permissions. We identify missing attachments; we do not produce or certify those technical documents. We never use your logins, design trails or determine that a permit or permission is unnecessary.

What we need from you

The project description and location, landowners, cost estimates or quotes, match sources, contact details and UEI status. A one-page summary is enough to discuss fit. We then agree the scope and complete list of inputs before starting.

Timing

Send your project summary by Monday 9 November 2026 so we can assess fit and leave time for revisions. Our target turnaround is five working days after we confirm that the agreed inputs are complete. Before accepting the work, we confirm availability and a delivery date in writing. The 9 November date is our intake cutoff, separate from FPR's 20 November deadline.

What this isn't

It isn't a promise of an invitation or of funding; FPR decides both. The full application is a separate stage, by invitation, and is not included in this service.
