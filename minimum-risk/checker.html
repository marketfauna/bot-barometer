25(b) label pre-check: findings against the federal conditions

A free pre-check for minimum-risk pesticide products claiming the exemption in 40 CFR 152.25(f). It compares your ingredients with the two federal tables and scans label text for claims the rule rules out. Rule data: 40 CFR 152.25 as published in the eCFR, current as of 2026-09-17; read by us on 19 September 2026. What you type is processed in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

About this section, its limits and who to contact

Use the name exactly as it appears on your label. The examples are invented. You do not need to enter a confidential formula: names and label percentages are enough.

List every inert by name; the rule requires each to be named on the label.

What this check is and is not. It is a mechanical comparison against the federal rule text and EPA's published guidance, with each finding cited so you can verify it. It cannot see your actual formula, judge whether a claim is misleading in context, or tell whether a food-use product needs a tolerance. A clean result here is not an EPA or state determination. Most states also require their own registration of 25(b) products, with their own documents, fees and label rules.
